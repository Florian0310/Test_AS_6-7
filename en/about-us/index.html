About us

Our story

Willy Schröder started training as an electromechanic and mechanical engineer in 1980, discovering his interest in quality tools.

The family business began in 2004. Building a home in the Piesting valley inspired a passion for professional equipment.

In 2008 this became Alles Saniert: making quality tools available to others through rental. Renting saves space, reduces costs and conserves resources.

Tools should be used rather than left gathering dust in a workshop, basement or garage.

Our services

We offer tool rental, advice and practical support for projects around the home, garden and woodland.

  • Tool rental: equipment for indoor and outdoor work.
  • Building materials: advice and suitable materials for lasting results.
  • Forestry: maintenance, thinning and professional pruning.

Accessibility

Our technical target is WCAG 2.2 AA. Navigation, search, forms and language selection support keyboard use and visible focus indicators. Reduced-motion preferences are respected.

Automated checks do not replace a complete assessment with assistive technology. Full conformance is not guaranteed. The Austrian BaFG’s applicability depends on the actual consumer service and any microenterprise exemption, which remain to be confirmed.

Report accessibility problems to office@allessaniert.at, including the affected page and assistive technology used.

Contact and rental inquiries

We process the submitted name, email, optional phone number and message, plus the equipment, requested dates and server-calculated price for rental inquiries. The purpose is handling your inquiry and possible pre-contractual steps (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR), or general communication based on legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). Required fields are identified on the form; without them the inquiry cannot be processed. Acknowledging this notice is not consent to marketing.

Rental inquiries are stored in the backend. Contact messages are sent through the configured email service. The operator must still confirm the providers, locations, retention periods and processing agreements. Automated deletion is not configured; a fixed six-month retention period is therefore not promised.

Operation and abuse prevention

Technical connection data is processed to deliver the site. Public submissions are rate limited. The hosting provider, actual server logs, retention and possible international transfers must be disclosed before publication. Application code does not establish that hosting logs are anonymised.

Cloudflare Turnstile is included on the contact form when a website key is configured. This creates connections to Cloudflare. The legal basis, provider information, processing agreement and possible international transfers must be assessed before activation. No website key is currently configured.

Local preferences and analytics

Your consent choice is stored as as_cookie_consent_v2 for 180 days. Explicitly selected language are stored locally; the return page from cookie settings is stored only for the session. You may clear these entries in your browser. Search terms and form entries are not saved in local storage.

Optional Google analytics loads only when IDs are configured and prior consent is given (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR; section 165(3) Austrian TKG 2021). No analytics IDs are currently configured. Before activation, provider details, purposes, cookie durations, recipients and transfer safeguards must be completed. Withdraw consent at any time in Cookie settings. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of earlier processing.

Your rights

Subject to the GDPR, you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, and may withdraw consent. You can complain to the Austrian Data Protection Authority. Rental contracts are not decided by automated processing.

When you explicitly switch language, current form entries may be transferred temporarily through session storage. They are read once and removed on the destination page, and are not restored after ten minutes.

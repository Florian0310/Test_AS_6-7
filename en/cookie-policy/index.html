Cookie policy

Necessary local preferences remember your consent and explicitly selected language and theme. The site uses no external fonts.

Optional analytics is off by default. Without configured Google IDs, Google tracking is not loaded. After consent and configuration, Google Tag Manager or Google Analytics may load; the implementation avoids starting both for the same setup.

Consent expires after 180 days. Invalid or old grants do not enable tracking. Withdrawal clears accessible Google analytics cookies and reloads the document to stop loaded trackers. This website cannot itself remove third-party cookies on other domains.
